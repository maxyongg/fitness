# Goals

**Status: draft, unconfirmed.** Max has never stated these. They are inferred from the
repo — the injury he is managing, the lifts he chases, the sessions he protects — and
written down so there is something concrete to correct. Every line below is a proposal
until he says otherwise. Cut what is wrong, add what is missing.

This file exists because the project drifted. `plan.md` says what the training is;
nothing said what it was *for*, which makes it impossible to judge whether the
programme is working or merely being followed.

## The candidates

### 1. Get the right lat back — the one that governs everything

The dominant thread. Rowing went 80kg (Jul 2025) → a form change in Sep 2025 → an
eight-month gap → one session at 50kg on 20 Aug 2026. Under physiotherapy care since
June 2026.

- **Near term:** establish whether the 50kg ceiling is pain-limited or caution-limited.
  This needs `pain(R)` logged on every pull day. It has never once been recorded.
- **Then:** move the right side on the green-light rules in `plan.md` — three greens
  held over two weeks, +2.5kg.
- **Success looks like:** loaded rowing that does not cost him the next morning.

The physio outranks this file. Nothing here is a reason to push through pain.

### 2. Stay available for football

Two hours of 7-a-side on Tuesday, sometimes Friday too. It is fixed, it is the thing
the rest schedules around, and it is the only leg training that reliably happens.

**Success looks like:** never missing a match because of something the gym did.

### 3. Keep the presses moving

- **Incline bench** is at an all-time high — 82.3 e1RM, currently working 60kg × 8.
  This is the lift that is working. Keep it working.
- **Overhead press** is the hole: 61.7 e1RM in February, 50.7 now. It regressed because
  it has no fixed home — push slot 2 keeps going to the Arnold or the machine.
  **Proposal:** give OHP slot 2 for a full block and see if it comes back.

### 4. Pull-ups: 27 fresh, then weighted

27 reps at position 1 on 27 Aug is the best in the record. Hold that across three
sessions, then re-add 5kg. This is also the cleanest test of the position finding.

### 5. Run the programme he designed

The fortnight asks for eight lifting sessions. The last twelve weeks average 2.67 a
week — about five and a half. The gap is the Friday Push and the Sunday session.

Not stated as a target because it may be that the template is more than he wants, and
that is a legitimate answer. But it is the single largest difference between the plan
and the reality, so it belongs on the list until he rules on it.

## Not goals, as far as the repo shows

Written down so they are not quietly assumed:

- **Not a bodybuilding split.** No weight target, no measurements, no photos anywhere.
- **Not leg strength.** Legs are 6.9% of every set ever logged and that share has been
  stable for two years. Once a fortnight is the design, monthly is the practice, and
  he has said not to push it.
- **Not more training.** Nothing suggests he wants a fourth or fifth session a week
  beyond what the template already asks for.

## What is missing

Things a goals document should say that nothing in this repo can answer:

- Is there a **date** anything is aimed at — a season, a trip, a birthday?
- Is the aim **strength**, **size**, **staying injury-free**, or **football
  performance**? These pull in different directions when they conflict, and knowing
  the priority order is what lets a programme make trade-offs without asking.
- What would make him call a block **successful** three months from now?
