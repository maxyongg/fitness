# The programme

Two-week rotation. Week A has one football session, Week B has two.
Order within each session is the order to perform them in — this is not cosmetic.

Sessions run 5–6 exercises because that is what fits the hour — it is **not** a tested
optimum. See `docs/findings.md`.

## The row protocol

Left and right are separate exercises that share a machine.

**Left** — progress normally. Note the 70kg figure predates the September 2025 form
change: the only 2026 row session (20 Aug) put *both* sides in at 50kg. Re-establish
where the left actually is before assuming a 20kg gap. Holding it back detrains a healthy lat for
nothing, and cross-education means training the good side produces measurable
strength gains in the injured one.

**Right** — start at the heaviest genuinely pain-free load, possibly 40kg.
Three green lights held for two consecutive weeks → +2.5kg. Any red → back to the
last green load and stay there.

| Green light | Means |
|---|---|
| Pain ≤ 3/10 during the set | Present, not sharp, doesn't change how you move |
| Baseline within 24h | Next morning feels like any other |
| Not creeping week to week | Compare to a fortnight ago, not yesterday |

This framework comes from Achilles tendinopathy research, not muscle strain. It is
an adaptation and the physio should sign off on it.

## Progression rules

- Incline bench, OHP, RDL — +2.5kg at the top of the range on every set
- Anything the right lat touches — reps before weight, always
- Pull-ups — back to 27 reps fresh; re-add 5kg once that holds three sessions
- Lateral raises — stay 15–20, +1kg at 20 across all three sets
- Nordics — 2×4 → 3×5 → 3×6, then slow the descent. Never add weight.

## When life interferes

- Restart at **Monday**, never Friday
- After 10+ days off, take 10% off top sets for the first session
- Travelling: Sunday's session needs a pull-up bar and a floor

## What this rests on

The pull-up position rule and the separate left/right row loads come out of his own
data and hold up. The 5–6 exercise session length does **not** — it is a scheduling
habit, not a finding. Working, and the open questions, in `docs/findings.md`.
